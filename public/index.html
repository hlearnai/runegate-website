How to play

1

Pick your secret Key

Draw a card from the Main Deck. Its rune is your Key, hidden from everyone else.

2

Build your Portal

Place runes into your Portal. Each rune lights a crystal. Light every rune in play to activate it.

3

Cast spells

Put two runes in your Spell area to cast one of 28 spells: attack, steal, shield, spy and more.

4

Unlock and win

With your Portal activated and a rune matching your Key in hand, unlock the Portal. First one through wins.

Cosmic Events

Switch them on before a game and the sky fights back. Every few rounds a random event hits every player at once.

Asteroid Field

Burning rocks rain down: every rune waiting in a Spell area is destroyed. Only a Seal protects it.

Black Hole

Swallows every player's whole hand. Nobody holds a card until they draw again.

Gravity Surge

Tears away every secret Key and replaces it with a new one from the Main Deck.

Sun Flare

Burns away every Shield, Fortify, Cloak, Seal and Mirror on the table.

Radiation Belts

The fullest Portals lose a crystal, unless a Shield soaks up the blast.
